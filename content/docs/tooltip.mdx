## Delay

`delayDuration` is the wait before the tooltip *starts* opening, not the wait
before the reader can see it. The 160ms entrance (the fade, the 4px slide and
the zoom) runs after it, and only the sum of the two is perceptible, so the
40ms default is a 200ms budget with that entrance already taken out. Set it to
200 and a tooltip arrives 360ms after the pointer does.
