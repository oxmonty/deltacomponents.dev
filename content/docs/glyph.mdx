The check isn't drawn on top of the rosette in a matching color — it's cut
out of it with an SVG `mask`, leaving a genuine hole. Painting the check
white (or whatever the surrounding background happens to be) only works on a
flat card; the moment the badge sits over a photo or a gradient, the paint
seams. A real hole never does, because there's nothing to match.

## Accessible label

<ComponentPreview name="glyph-label" />

By default the badge is `aria-hidden` — it's meant to sit beside a name or
title that already announces "verified". Pass `label` to announce it instead,
which switches the badge to `role="img"` with an `<svg><title>`.
